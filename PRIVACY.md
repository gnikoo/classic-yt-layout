Privacy Policy — Classic YT Layout

Last updated: March 2026

Classic YT Layout does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information of any kind.

The extension:
- Makes no network requests
- Stores only a single on/off preference locally in your browser using the browser's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local)
- Does not access, read, or transmit any data from the pages it runs on
- Does not use analytics, tracking, or crash reporting of any kind

The locally stored on/off preference never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
